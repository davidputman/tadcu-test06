# Tadcu — Episode 6: "The Latch"

An episode of the **Tadcu** animated cartoon series. Tadcu (Welsh for
"Grandpa"), his sheepdog Bryn, a twelve-pound dream from Aberystwyth, and
the gate — which has been the canniest character in the show since
Episode 1. Season finale of the first test run.

restart, scene skip, click the bar to scrub by scene, keys 1–6 to jump to a
scene.

## Story so far

Episodes 1–5 live in `../test01`–`../test05`. The gate: opened (ep1),
latched (ep2), judged (ep3), built into a wall (ep4), left wide open for a
triumphant return (ep5). This week, technology.

## Episode 6 summary

A crate arrives from JONES LOCKS, ABERYSTWYTH: a self-latching gate latch
("opens and closes on its own, like a bank"). Installation goes well —
Bryn signs the crate with a paw — the latch shines, the girls are shown
the demonstration, and nine seconds later Bryn nose-lifts it and pops the
gate like a hare opening a lunchbox. Dusk: two friends, one bench, the
gate wide open, one kettle pouring.

- **TADCU:** "Nos da — and never change."

## New in this episode

- **The latch** — brass, self-latching, technically undefeated.
- **The crate** — cardboard, string, and structural honesty.
- **The bench** — the two leads' contract moment.
