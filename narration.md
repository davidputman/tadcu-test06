# Tadcu — Episode 6: "The Latch"
## Narration (voice-over) script — recording copy

*One narrator, same voice. Finale register: unhurried, fond, resigned —
the sound of a man telling you the ending while the ending behind him
reopens the gate. Music bed: harp, with one low railway-bell for the
latch.*

Character lines stay as on-screen subtitle bubbles, in *italics* under
each beat for the editor only. **BOLD = narrator, to be recorded.**
